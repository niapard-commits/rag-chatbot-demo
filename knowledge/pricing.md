# Pricing

All prices are sample data in EUR.

## Price list
- Check-up: 40 EUR
- Professional cleaning with polishing: 70 EUR
- Teeth whitening (in-office): 220 EUR
- White composite filling: from 60 EUR
- Orthodontic consultation with scan: 50 EUR
- Clear aligners: individual quote after consultation

## Payment
We accept cards and cash. Payment is due after the visit. Treatment plans above 500 EUR can be paid in up to six monthly instalments without interest.

## Insurance
We work with public insurance for basic treatments. Cosmetic procedures such as whitening are not covered.
