# Services

Bright Smile Dental is a sample (fictional) dental clinic used to demo this chatbot.

## Check-up and cleaning
A routine check-up takes about 30 minutes. Professional cleaning with polishing takes about 45 minutes. We recommend a check-up every six months.

## Teeth whitening
In-office whitening takes about 60 minutes and lightens teeth by several shades. It is not recommended during pregnancy. A short consultation is required first.

## Fillings
We use modern white composite fillings. A simple filling takes 30 to 60 minutes.

## Orthodontics
We offer clear aligners and classic braces. The first consultation includes a scan and a treatment plan. It takes about 45 minutes.

## Emergency care
If you have severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth, call the clinic during opening hours and we will fit you in the same day when possible.
