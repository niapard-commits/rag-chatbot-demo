# Frequently asked questions

## What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00. On Saturday and Sunday the clinic is closed. Emergency calls are answered during opening hours.

## Where are you located?
Our clinic is at 12 Example Street, Bratislava (sample address). The address and location are also on our website.

## Do I need an appointment?
Yes, please book in advance. You can book with this assistant by giving your name, contact details, the service and a date and time.

## How can I cancel or move my appointment?
Please tell us at least 24 hours before the visit. Late cancellations may be charged 20 EUR.

## Do you treat children?
Yes. Children from 3 years old are welcome. A parent or guardian must stay with a child under 15.

## Do you speak English?
Yes. Our team speaks Slovak, English and Ukrainian.

## What should I bring to the first visit?
Bring an ID, your insurance card and, if you have them, earlier X-rays.
